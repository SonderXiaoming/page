## Community-Built Software

Much of my open-source work grew out of the **Princess Connect Re:Dive** community. Kanna Connection Re:Dive, for example, turned game data into automated updates for roughly **100 group chats**. At around 30 members per group, that is an estimated audience of about **3,000 people**.

That scale taught me more than how to call an API. It meant thinking about concurrency, unreliable upstream responses, duplicate notifications, regional differences, and the small workflow details that determine whether people actually enjoy using a bot.

A few projects that represent this side of my work:

- **[Kanna Connection Re:Dive 2](https://github.com/SonderXiaoming/kanna_connection_redive_2)** — the next generation of the monitoring service, with a WebUI, permission controls, arena tools, and multi-region support.
- **[PCR Arena Companion 2](https://github.com/SonderXiaoming/pcrjjc_huannai2)** — arena lookups with multi-UID binding, account-specific alerts, private or group delivery, and CAPTCHA handling.
- **[Kanna Note](https://github.com/SonderXiaoming/kanna_note)** — quick game-information lookups backed by Python, SQL, and automatic data updates.
- **[PCR Login Helper](https://github.com/SonderXiaoming/pcr_login_helper)** — a Rust, Tauri, and ADB desktop utility for several regional game servers.
- **[Misora Note](https://github.com/SonderXiaoming/MisoraNote)** — a Flutter companion app that brings the same quick-reference idea to mobile devices.

The [Projects](/projects/) page has the broader collection. Over time, I also want to write down more of the stories behind Kanna and the JJC tools—not only what they do, but why their design evolved the way it did.
